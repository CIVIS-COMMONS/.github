# ORIGO CIVIS · CIVIS Commons

**Build openly. Observe honestly. Steward responsibly.**

CIVIS Commons is an open-source initiative dedicated to responsible technology, independent evaluation, and the preservation of shared knowledge.

Operating under the proposed ORIGO CIVIS identity, we aim to make software and technical systems easier to inspect, understand, evaluate, and maintain.

## Our Purpose

We believe technology should remain understandable to the people who depend on it.

Our work focuses on cultivating open technologies and shared knowledge through independent research, transparent evaluation, useful documentation, and responsible stewardship.

We seek to preserve the freedom to inspect, understand, adapt, and responsibly maintain the systems we depend upon.

## Our Principles

- **Openness:** Favor inspectable source code, accessible documentation, and transparent methods.
- **Independence:** Protect the ability of projects and contributors to make their own decisions.
- **Privacy:** Minimize unnecessary data collection and respect user sovereignty.
- **Evidence:** Prefer reproducible findings and clearly documented limitations over unsupported claims.
- **Stewardship:** Maintain projects responsibly without treating ownership as unlimited authority.
- **Continuity:** Preserve intent and context while allowing projects to evolve.

## What We Do

Our initial areas of work include:

- Open-source software development and maintenance.
- Independent testing and responsible technology evaluation.
- Privacy-conscious design and review.
- Technical documentation and knowledge preservation.
- Public resources that help people understand the tools they use.

## Our First Pilot

**SPROUT** is our initial practical example of privacy-conscious, local-first software development.

We intend to use its development and testing to practice transparent review, reproducible checks, and responsible documentation.

Project findings will reflect the scope of the work actually performed. Participation in CIVIS Commons does not automatically constitute a security certification or endorsement.

## Participation

We welcome thoughtful examination, constructive criticism, reproducible bug reports, and contributions that align with our principles.

Contributors retain the rights and responsibilities established by each project's license and contribution policies. Participation does not grant automatic ownership or governance authority over other projects.

## A Living Commitment

CIVIS Commons is intended to grow through useful work, transparent decisions, and responsible stewardship.

We do not seek to eliminate change. We seek to make change understandable, intentional, and accountable.

**Observe honestly. Distill carefully. Preserve faithfully. Reflect humbly. Return generously.**
